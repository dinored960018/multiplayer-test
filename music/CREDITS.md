# SKULLDRIFT soundtrack

Every track here is **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**, taken from OpenGameArt.org on 2026-11-12. No attribution is required;
the composers are credited here anyway. Each file is the original re-encoded for the game: loudness evened out
(−16 LUFS, peaks limited to −1 dB), 96 kbps stereo MP3, two-part tracks joined (intro + loop, `loop` in
`game/src/audio.js` `MUSIC` is where the loop starts).
